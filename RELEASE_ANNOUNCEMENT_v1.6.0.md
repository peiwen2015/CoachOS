# CoachOS v1.6.0 正式發布

## 中文

CoachOS v1.6.0 是 Comparison Intelligence 版本。

這一版不再只把多堂活動排在一起，而是先建立可重現、可解釋的相似活動集合，再依整堂活動、品質主段與 Strides 分層比較。CoachOS 現在可以看見：同一類訓練是否維持相近輸出、主段後半成本是否改變、Strides 是否符合目標角色，以及逐組刺激是否一致。

平台也新增 Conditional Baseline、metric-level anomaly evidence、execution pattern、role fit、rep consistency 與近期行為重複摘要。這些結果都保留資料來源與判讀邊界，避免把統計偏離直接等同於訓練做錯，也不把未驗證的觀察壓縮成單一分數。

v1.6.0 的核心產品方向是：

**平台先辨識訓練行為，AI 再解釋訓練意義。**

## English

CoachOS v1.6.0 is the Comparison Intelligence release.

This version moves activity comparison beyond side-by-side data. CoachOS now builds deterministic, explainable Similar Activities sets and compares whole-activity cost, primary-work execution, and Strides as separate stimulus layers. It can identify whether a workout remains stable, how the second half changes, whether Strides fit their intended role, and whether individual reps are consistent.

The release adds Conditional Baselines, metric-level anomaly evidence, execution patterns, role fit, rep consistency, and recent behavior summaries. Evidence provenance and assessment boundaries remain explicit: statistical deviation is not automatically treated as a training error, and observations are not compressed into an unexplainable score.

The core product principle of v1.6.0 is:

**CoachOS identifies training behavior first; AI explains what it may mean.**
